Our Story

African Story Village began long before it was a company. Its founder grew up listening to his father, the late Mr. Ogbuagu Moses Azubuike, tell captivating stories to his six children in the Igbo language — stories that shaped how the founder came to see imagination, character and culture.

In 2018, that love of storytelling became a free WordPress project featuring African-themed prose and poetry. The project paused around 2020 while the founder relocated to Awka for engineering education. The vision didn't disappear — it evolved, eventually becoming a children's storytelling and publishing brand focused on imagination, learning, character and African cultural heritage.

Vision

To become the foremost African children's story brand — building a generation of imaginative, observant, morally grounded, and intellectually curious children through the power of purposeful storytelling.

Mission

To create beautifully crafted, culturally rooted story books and digital content that actively develop creativity, observation, attention, vocabulary, and moral character in children aged 3 to 14 — making world-class African stories accessible to every child, everywhere.
